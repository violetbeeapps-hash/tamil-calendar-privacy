Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how தியா Calendar ("the App"), developed by VioletBee, handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

தியா Calendar does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The App does not currently collect or store information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, or account credentials.

2. Calendar and App Data

The App provides Tamil calendar information including Tamil dates, Tamil months, Tamil years, festivals, holidays, Panchangam-related information, sunrise and sunset times, Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, and other calendar information.

This information is provided for informational and general reference purposes.

3. Notifications

The App may provide optional local notifications for daily calendar reminders.

Notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. You can enable or disable notification permissions through the App or your device settings.

The App does not require your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to provide these local reminders.

4. Gold Rate Information

The App may retrieve gold rate information from an external third-party API service to display current or recent gold price information.

The App does not intentionally send personal information to the gold rate service as part of these requests.

The availability and accuracy of gold rate information may depend on the external service.

Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, request information, and other information necessary to provide and protect its services. Such processing is subject to Cloudflare's own privacy practices.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may communicate with third-party services to provide specific functionality, such as retrieving gold rate information and delivering application content.

The App does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or advertising services such as Google AdMob.

7. Advertising

The current version of தியா Calendar does not display advertisements.

If advertising or additional third-party services are introduced in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect the applicable data practices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information involved in providing the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the App does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information, children can use the calendar features without providing personal information to us.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or a user database for the current version of the App. Therefore, we do not retain personal account information belonging to users.
